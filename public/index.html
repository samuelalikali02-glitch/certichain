Quick actions

Wallet information

Verify Certificate

Check whether an exact certificate document has been registered on the blockchain.

Upload document

Select the original PDF or image certificate. CertiChain calculates its SHA-256 fingerprint and checks the blockchain and metadata database.

Supported formats: PDF, PNG and JPEG. Maximum size: 10 MB.

Register Certificate

Upload a certificate to IPFS and register its fingerprint through your authorized Ethereum wallet.

Issuer registration

Only the administrator and authorized institutions can register certificates. The original document is uploaded to Pinata IPFS, its SHA-256 fingerprint is registered on Ethereum, and its metadata is saved in the database.

Connect an authorized wallet to access registration.

Supported formats: PDF, PNG and JPEG. Maximum size: 10 MB.

Revoke certificate

An administrator or the original issuing institution can revoke a registered certificate.

Institution Management

Manage institutions permitted to register certificates on CertiChain.

Authorize institution

Enter the institution's public Ethereum wallet address. Only the contract administrator can perform this action.

Remove institution access

Remove an institution's permission to register new certificates.
